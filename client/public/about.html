About muve

Moving Nigeria, one ride at a time.

muve is a full-stack, real-time ride-hailing platform built from scratch for Nigerian streets. From Lagos to Abuja, we're connecting riders with trusted drivers — making every journey safer, faster, and fairer.

Our story

muve was born from a simple frustration: getting across Lagos shouldn't be complicated. The danfo buses run, the okadas weave, but when you need a ride that's reliable, safe, and priced fairly — the options are thin. So we built one.

What started as a clone-the-architecture project became a real platform: a full-stack ride-hailing system with real-time GPS tracking, dynamic surge pricing, a matching engine that finds the nearest driver in seconds, and a simulated fleet that drives real road routes. No black boxes. No opaque pricing. Just a clean, honest ride from A to B.

Today muve runs as a demo you can try end-to-end — sign up as a rider, request a trip, and watch a driver pick you up and drive the route in real time. It's the full experience, built to scale.

What we believe

Built for Nigeria

Naira pricing, Lagos defaults, local car models, and plates that look like home. Not a foreign app retrofitted — built ground-up for our streets.

How a muve ride works

You set your destination

Step 1

Search an address or tap the map. muve geocodes it instantly and shows the road distance and duration.

Pick your tier

Step 2

MuveX for everyday rides, MuveXL for groups, or Muve Black for premium. Fares shown upfront in Naira — no surprises.

The matching engine finds your driver

Step 3

Your ride is offered to the nearest idle driver. If they decline or timeout, it cascades to the next closest — until someone accepts.

Track in real time

Step 4

Watch your driver drive to you along real roads, then to your destination. GPS streams every second over WebSocket.

Rate and tip

Step 5

Arrive, rate your driver, leave a tip. Ratings aggregate onto driver profiles — quality rises, poor service doesn't last.

Built with

React 18 and Vite on the frontend with Leaflet maps. Node, Express, and Socket.io on the backend with better-sqlite3 for zero-latency queries. OSRM for road routing, Nominatim for geocoding, OpenStreetMap tiles for the map — all free, no API keys. Deployable as a single process on Render or any Node host.
