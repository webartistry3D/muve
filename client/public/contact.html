Frequently asked

How do I start riding?

Go to the muve app, sign up as a rider, set your pickup and destination, pick a tier, and request. A driver will be matched and on the way in seconds.

How do I become a driver?

Sign up as a driver on the platform, enter your vehicle details (make, model, plate, color), and tap GO to go online. You'll receive ride offers with a 15-second accept window. Email drivers@muve.app for onboarding support.

Is muve available outside Lagos?

The demo currently defaults to Lagos, but the platform works anywhere with OpenStreetMap coverage. The architecture is location-agnostic — expanding to other cities is a configuration change, not a rebuild.

How are fares calculated?

Base fare + (distance × per-km rate) + (time × per-min rate), multiplied by surge if active. All rates are in Naira and shown before you request. MuveX starts at ₦500 minimum, MuveXL at ₦800, Muve Black at ₦1,500.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Authentication uses JWT tokens stored locally. The code is open source — you can inspect exactly how your data is handled at github.com/xSwaraJx/muve.
